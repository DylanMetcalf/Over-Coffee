# Over Coffee — how to use it

## 5-minute first run
1. Open the link. Tap **Create an account** (email + 8-character password).
2. Type what you care about, in your own words (“AI”, “hazardous area electrical”, “restaurants in Cape Town”). Tick the areas you want, or tap **Broad topic only**.
3. Pick **5, 10 or 20 minutes** and **Business** or **Conversational**.
4. Choose when it should be ready each morning (email) and tap Continue. Your first edition is built while you watch.
5. Tap **Read today’s newspaper**. **Swipe sideways** to turn the page, **scroll down** to read it.
6. Reached the last page? You’re done for today. That’s the point.
7. Add it to your home screen (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android/desktop Chrome: Install).

## Full guide
**What it is.** A personal daily newspaper. It reads the news for your topics, groups duplicate reports into one story, checks claims against sources, and gives you one finite edition. It stops when it has told you what matters.

**Creating an account.** Email + password. Stay logged in on your own devices; use *Settings → Log out* on shared ones.

**Setting up interests.** Type anything. Common topics are recognised; anything else becomes a new topic the engine researches as it comes. For a new topic you’ll see suggested areas (e.g. explosion protection, zone classification). Select any, add your own, or follow the broad topic only. Edit anytime in **My topics**.

**Reading time.** 5 min = only what’s worth five minutes (can be less on a quiet day). 10 = the full picture. 20 = more context. It never pads.

**Edition style.** *Business*: concise, precise, calm. *Conversational*: simple words and a friend’s tone, same facts, same sources, same caveats. Switch any time (top of the paper, or Settings); it never re-researches.

**First edition.** Takes a short while the first time (research + checking + writing). After that, a new edition is prepared each morning at your chosen time; opening the app before then builds it on demand.

**Reading on a phone.** Each page is a section (front page, the lead, one per topic, done). Swipe left/right to turn pages, or use the arrows at the bottom. Scroll up/down inside a page. The page bar shows where you are. Your place is remembered: close the app, come back, you’re on the same page.

**Learn more.** Under any story: expanded context, a timeline, and sources grouped by what each one contributed, with a path to the original. **Back to the newspaper** returns you to the page you were on.

**Today only.** *Change today* lets you swap topics for one day, try a **Random topic**, or **Feeling adventurous** (one step beyond your interests) without touching your settings.

**PDF.** *PDF* at the top of any edition (or the Download page) gives a printable paper.

**Changing preferences.** Settings: reading time, voice, PDF size, delivery time and email. Topics: My topics.

**Troubleshooting**
- *“Please log in” keeps appearing:* your session ended; log in again.
- *First edition is slow or empty:* some topics have little news; broaden the topic or add a related one. A quiet topic is reported honestly as quiet.
- *No email:* check spam; email needs the host to have Resend configured (ask whoever runs the server).
- *Can’t install the app:* iPhone needs Safari; Chrome/Edge elsewhere.
- *Page won’t swipe on a computer:* the swipe layout is phone-only; on desktop it scrolls normally.

**Report a bug.** Send: what you tapped, what you expected, what happened, a screenshot, your phone/browser, and the date on the edition. One line is enough.

**MVP vs later.** *Now:* accounts, any-topic research, two voices, phone pages, sources, PDF, email, install, offline last edition. *Later:* push and WhatsApp pings, audio file, password reset, sharing, archive search, full-article retrieval, licensed news feeds.
